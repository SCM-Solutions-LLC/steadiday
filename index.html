If your parent falls, you’ll know.

SteadiDay is an iPhone app for your mom or dad. It watches for hard falls and reminds them about their medicines, and when something goes wrong it texts you where they are.

See How It Works

What’s in the app

These are real screens from SteadiDay. Fall detection and medication reminders are on the free plan.

Free

Fall Detection

Turn on Always-on Protection once and it stays on, with no session to remember to start. SteadiDay watches for hard falls in the background, and if one is detected with no response within 30 seconds, your trusted contacts are texted automatically with your location.

Needs a free SteadiDay account to send the texts, and iPhone Location set to Always so the app keeps watching in the background. If either is missing, the app tells you protection is limited rather than letting you assume you are covered.

Free

Medication Reminders

Scan the label or type the name, pick the times, and SteadiDay reminds them. Every dose they check off is recorded, and Family Link can share that record with you.

Free covers up to 5 medications and 7 reminders. Premium removes both limits.

Premium

Family Link

Your parent can share a private Care Summary with you: the week’s medication streak, today’s medicines and reminders, the daily check-in and their step count. It’s a web link, so you don’t install anything or make an account, and they can switch it off whenever they like.

Premium

Steady Moves

Short balance and strength routines based on the CDC’s STEADI fall-prevention program. Most are done from a sturdy chair, with spoken instructions and large text.

Two home screens to choose from

Simple Mode is the default. Standard Mode adds tabs for everything else. You can switch between them any time in Settings.

Setup takes about five minutes

  1. Download the app

    Get SteadiDay from the App Store and pick Simple or Standard Mode. You can switch later.

  2. Add the people to tell

    Add whoever should hear if something happens, then create the free account SteadiDay needs to text them. It takes a minute.

  3. Add medicines

    Scan a medication label or type the name, and choose when the reminders go off.

  4. Turn on protection

    Switch on Always-on Protection and leave it on. Fall detection then runs in the background, and SOS is one tap from the home screen.

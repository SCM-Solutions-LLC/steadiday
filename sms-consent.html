SMS Consent and Opt-In

Last Updated: November 18, 2026

1. Who sends these texts

The short version: SteadiDay, from SCM Solutions LLC, texts the trusted contacts a SteadiDay user picks: family members or caregivers. Texts are fall alerts, test alerts, and, only for contacts who reply YES, daily check-in updates. We never send marketing, and we never share or sell phone numbers. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help.

2. How consent is collected

A trusted contact is added inside the SteadiDay iPhone app:

  1. The user opens Trusted Contacts (or the contacts step during setup) and enters the contact's name and mobile number, or picks them from the phone's contacts.
  2. Before the contact can be saved, the user must tick a required checkbox that reads:
    "[Contact name] has agreed to get emergency alert texts from SteadiDay."
  3. The contact can't be saved until the box is ticked. The app records the time it was ticked.
  4. If the contact's number is changed later, the box must be ticked again.

3. The first text a contact gets

As soon as the contact is saved, SteadiDay texts them this confirmation:

You have been added as an emergency contact in SteadiDay by [user's name]. You will receive SMS alerts if an emergency is detected (such as a fall), and check-in updates or missed check-in alerts if they turn those on. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. SCM Solutions LLC.

4. Check-in texts need the contact's own YES

If the user turns on daily check-in updates or missed check-in alerts, SteadiDay first sends each contact one invitation:

SteadiDay: [user's name] would like to send you a daily check-in text, and a reminder if they miss a check-in. Reply YES to agree. Until you do, you will not get these texts. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

No check-in texts are sent to a contact until they reply YES.

5. What contacts receive, and how often

  • Fall alerts: when the app detects a possible fall and the user doesn't respond. Each alert includes the user's name, their location as map links and, when available, a live-location link. One text per contact per incident.
  • Test alerts: when the user sends one. They are clearly marked as tests.
  • Check-in updates and missed check-in reminders: usually one a day, only after the contact replies YES.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. SteadiDay does not charge for texts.

6. Opting out and getting help

  • STOP: reply STOP (or UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END or QUIT) to stop texts from that number. You'll get one confirmation, and no further texts from that number.
  • START: reply START to resubscribe.
  • HELP: reply HELP, or email support@steadiday.com.

The number that sends these texts can't take calls. If you get a fall alert, call the person directly, or call 911 in an emergency.

7. Privacy

Mobile numbers and opt-in information are not shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. For details, see our Privacy Policy, Section 10 and our Terms of Service, Section 12.
